Privacy Policy for Evangelism Tracker

Developer: HIS.dev
Effective date: September 12, 2026

HIS.dev provides Evangelism Tracker as an offline-first ministry productivity application. This Privacy Policy explains how Evangelism Tracker accesses, stores, uses, and handles information within the current version of the app.

1. Information stored in the app

Depending on how Evangelism Tracker is used, the app may store information entered by the user, including person records, evangelism records, follow-up progress, schedules, activity details, lesson progress, notes, app preferences, and locally selected photos or other attachments.

2. Local, offline-first storage

Evangelism Tracker is currently designed to work offline. App records are stored locally on the user's Android device. The current version does not require a user account, does not use cloud synchronization, and does not send ministry records to a HIS.dev server.

3. Data collection and sharing

HIS.dev does not collect, sell, rent, or share user-entered Evangelism Tracker records with third parties. The current version does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a remote backend service for user data.

When a user deliberately exports, backs up, prints, or shares information from the app, the user chooses the destination or recipient. Those user-initiated actions are controlled by the user and may involve other apps or storage providers selected by the user.

4. Permissions and device access

Evangelism Tracker may use Android permissions and system capabilities that support app functions, such as notifications, alarms, vibration, and restoring scheduled reminders after the device restarts. These capabilities are used to provide reminder and scheduling features and are not used by HIS.dev to collect user data.

When the user selects a local file, photo, PDF, backup file, or export destination through Android system interfaces, Evangelism Tracker may access that selected item only as needed to perform the requested app function.

5. Backup and export

Evangelism Tracker can create backup or export files when the user chooses to do so. These files may contain app records and, where applicable, user-selected photos or attachments. Backup and export files remain under the user's control and are stored wherever the user chooses to save or share them.

6. Security

Evangelism Tracker keeps its working data within the app's local storage on the user's device. Device-level security, screen locks, operating-system protections, and the user's handling of exported or backup files affect the security of that information. HIS.dev does not receive or maintain a server-side copy of the current app's user records.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data remains in Evangelism Tracker until the user edits, deletes, clears, restores over, or otherwise changes it using available app features, or until the app's local data is removed from the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored app information.

Copies that the user has separately exported, backed up, printed, or shared are outside the app's local storage and must be managed or deleted by the user from the location where those copies were saved.

8. Children and intended audience

Evangelism Tracker is intended for adult users involved in ministry, evangelism, follow-up, and related organizational work. It is not designed as a children's app.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces features such as account sign-in, cloud synchronization, online collaboration, analytics, or other services that change how data is handled, HIS.dev will update this Privacy Policy and the corresponding Google Play Data Safety information as appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or concerns about Evangelism Tracker, contact HIS.dev at iammanniey07@gmail.com.
